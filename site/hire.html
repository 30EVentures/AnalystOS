Hire an AnalystOS role

Describe the problem, pick the role, answer a few scoping questions, and get back finished work in the format the role's work is read in: a memo, a workbook with live formulas, or a deck. Every number in it is printed from a figure traced to your own words, a stated calculation, or a labelled assumption.
