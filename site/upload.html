Live preview

Upload a document, get a cited report.

CSV, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, or PDF — any document, table-shaped or not. By default it's read as real text and analyzed by a model whose every number is independently checked against the source. Nothing you upload is stored; each request is processed and discarded. While it is processed, the text extracted from your document (and any images cropped from it) is sent to Anthropic’s API. Use non-confidential documents only.

Preview access is code-gated. Entered once per browser — remembered locally after a successful submit, never sent anywhere but this request.
