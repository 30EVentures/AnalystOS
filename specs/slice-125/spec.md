# Slice 125 - deck quality: what a board reader caught in the first live deck

## Goal

The third paid test delivered a deck whose every number traced, but a reader
would catch: "Three paths exist" over four options and "Six metrics" over five;
a roadmap whose board update came after the five-week deadline; timing and
proportion claims the data did not support ("immediately before", "a
disproportionate share"); price "refuted" where the evidence was mixed; no cost
put on the churn; the client's customers' size read as its own. These carry into
memos and workbooks, which draw the same content.

## What changes

1. **Code check, deliberately narrow:** an options, measures or risks title that
   opens by counting all its rows ("Three paths exist...", "Six metrics
   track...") must count them right; the repair request says how to fix it. It
   applies only when the count opens the title, is directly followed by the rows'
   noun, is the title's only counting word, and no subset word appears ("only",
   "other", "top", "key", "first", "most", "of", ...). The release review showed a
   wider check refused ordinary titles ("Two key risks could derail the plan",
   "Only one option meets the deadline"), each a paid repair call. Accepted
   misses: "There are three realistic options" or "Three strategic options" over
   four rows pass.
2. **Prompt quality rules:** put a cost on the problem as a computed figure when
   the client's numbers allow; fit the roadmap to the client's deadline; claim no
   timing or proportion the text does not support and describe the client only in
   its own words; supported or refuted only on clear evidence, mixed evidence is
   untested; title counts match their rows.

Not code-checked, stated: the deadline fit, unsupported claims, verdict
strength and the cost of the problem are judgements a check cannot make
without refusing good work (a verdict-needs-a-figure check would refuse the
churn worked example's sound, wordless evidence); they are prompt rules.

## Done when

1. The first live deck's two count titles are refused, with a fix in the message,
   and a correct second answer builds; the 16 worked examples and 48 natural
   variants still pass in one attempt.
2. The prompt carries every quality rule.
3. Full suite OK; refresh check clean; Flashy fence holds; no paid call.
